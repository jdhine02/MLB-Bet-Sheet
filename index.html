🎯 What's on Tab 1 — Overview

  • BOTD (Best of the Day) — Single top Hit prop + single top K prop. Tracked separately in results. Highest edge × model score wins selection.
  • Hit Props — Top 12 O0.5H plays. Gate: AVG≥.215, OBP≥.290, min 10 AB, no L vs L. Sorted by composite score. 2.5 units each.
  • HR Props — Top 12 HR plays. Gate: season HR≥3. Barrel%, EV, L5/L10 HR, park, platoon, wind. 2.5 units each.
  • TB 2+ Props — Top 12 total bases plays. Gate: L10 TB2+≥3, (TB2%+TB3+%)≥25%, min 10 AB. Sorted by L10 TB2+ × SLG.
  • K Props — All today's SPs with K9 data. Line set by K9 threshold (≤6.9=3.5, 7–9.9=4.5, ≥10=5.5) ± opponent K% adjustment. H/A K9 in column 14.
  • H+R+RBI Props — Top 12 O1.5 HRBI plays. Matchup grade shown right of Mom. HRBI L10 = max(L10H, L10R, L10RBI). O1.5 wins = H+R+RBI≥2.
  • Streak Table — Active hit streaks ≥2G, sorted by streak length.
  • Parlays — Safe (consensus locks) + Lotto (high-edge) two-leg parlays built from the day's top picks.

🏟 What's on Tabs 2 & 3 — Game Cards

  • SP Info Strip — ERA, K/9, BAA, L5 stats, Exp K (blended K9 40% season + 60% L5), park/weather factors.
  • Batter Table — Every confirmed batter with: matchup grade, Exp H/TB/R/RBI, L5H/L10H, L10HR, L10R, L10RBI, L10 HRBI, TB L5/L10 (2+ games), AVG, HR, H2H vs SP, VS Hand.
  • Lineup Header — ✅ CONFIRMED [TEAM] or 📋 PROJECTED. Team shown in team-color pill. Projected = verify MLB.com before betting.
  • NRFI Box — Combined score from both SP NRFI rates + team NRFI rates. PLAY ≥80%, sharp LEAN ≥73% (both SPs elite), WATCH 70–73%, SKIP below 70%.
  • Game Lines — ML odds, O/U line, model recommendation with reasoning.

📈 What's on Tab 4 — Results & History

  • BOTD Yesterday — Single Hit + single K result with grades.
  • SP K Actuals — Yesterday's starter K totals vs recommended line. Team pills for matchup.
  • Game Results — Score, NRFI result, both SPs. Team color pills.
  • Season Summary — W/L/Win%/Grade/P&L by bet type. Break-even: −110 = 52.4%, −120 = 54.5%, −130 = 56.5%, −150 = 60.0%.
  • Recent Bets — Last 3 days, all types. ML/OU/NRFI shown per game (not collapsed). Team pill right of player name. P&L: wins show total returned + profit in parens; losses show wager only.
  • Badges in Recent — 🔒✅➖⚠️✕ edge badges + 🔐❗🚨 consensus badges stored from build day and shown in history.
